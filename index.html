How it works: enter the patient's name, species, and weight at the top of any condition page. Each drug shows the label-chart dose pre-selected, with practical alternatives (including tablet-fraction options) below it. Check the medications to dispense, adjust frequencies, then use the Generated Instructions panel to copy a concise medical-record note or a formatted client discharge sheet — with checkboxes, feeding instructions, and follow-up text — ready to paste into your EMR or print.
Adding a new condition: upload a single conditions/yourcondition.json file — that's it. The index rebuilds itself automatically within a minute (GitHub Action), and the new card appears here. Use any existing JSON as a template; include "title", "desc", and "icon" fields for the card.
Internal use only. Compiled from practice dosing spreadsheets; includes off-label options used at the clinician's discretion. Verify all doses, concentrations, and label directions before administering or dispensing.
